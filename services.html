Private AI agents, running on your own infrastructure

Automation for the repetitive parts of your operations, deployed inside your perimeter rather than on a vendor's cloud.

Most AI tools ask you to send your working files to someone else's servers. For a firm handling client matters, loan applications or signed contracts, that is often a difficult conversation with your own compliance team — and sometimes simply not an option.

We deploy autonomous agents on hardware you control, using a model provider account in your own name. Your documents stay where they already are. We hold no copy, and no third-party automation platform sits in the middle.

What we automate

We do not sell an open-ended assistant. We pick two or three tasks that eat your team's week and automate exactly those, with the scope written down before we start.

Legal practices

Client intake triage, first-pass document summaries, and filing deadlines tracked against your matter list.

Lending and insurance

Application pre-screening, document checklist verification, and follow-ups that go out on schedule instead of when someone remembers.

Property firms

Enquiry routing to the right agent, listing data kept current across portals, and viewing coordination.

How we keep it contained

An agent with the run of your systems is a liability, not an asset. We document what it will not do as carefully as what it will.

  • No write access to production dataEvery deployment reads from your systems and writes only to its own workspace.
  • Nothing leaves without a humanOutbound email and messages are drafted for review. A person presses send.
  • Isolated executionThe agent runs in a container with a defined list of systems it can reach, and nothing else.
  • A hard ceiling on spendMonthly model usage is capped at a figure you set. You see the running total, because the account is yours.

What we run

We build on established open source rather than a platform of our own, so you are never locked into us. If you part ways with SoftWebLab, the deployment keeps working and any competent engineer can pick it up.

Hermes Agent

An open-source autonomous agent with persistent memory, sandboxed execution, and delivery to the messaging platforms your team already uses. We pin a fixed version per deployment and handle upgrades on a scheduled window rather than automatically.

MIT licensed

n8n

Visual workflow automation for the steps that should stay predictable, with connectors to mail, calendars and the systems you already run. The instance lives on your server, under your own credentials, and you can see every step it takes.
