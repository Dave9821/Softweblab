Aravind Terraces

Tower B · Whitefield, Bengaluru 560066

3 BHKReady to move7th of 14 floorsEast facingSemi-furnished

A three-bedroom unit in the second tower of a four-tower development off Whitefield Main Road. The flat sits on the seventh floor, east facing, which in this block means morning light in the living room and the master bedroom, and no direct afternoon sun on the west wall.

Carpet area is 1,180 sq ft against a built-up of 1,680 — a loading of about 30%, which is typical for this corridor and worth checking against anything you compare it with. The kitchen is plumbed for a dishwasher and has a separate utility balcony. Two of the three bedrooms have fitted wardrobes; the third does not.

The tower received its occupancy certificate, so there is no GST on this purchase. Maintenance runs at ₹3.80 per sq ft per month, billed quarterly by the resident association.
