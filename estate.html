Most portals hide the number that decides the deal.

Before the visit

Carpet, built-up, super built-up — all three

The gap between them is often 25%. Showing one number and calling it "area" is how buyers end up paying for a corridor.

During the visit

A floor plan you can read on a phone

Drawn to scale with room dimensions, not a photographed brochure page at 200 KB.

After the visit

The full cost, not the sticker price

Base price, floor rise, parking, GST, registration and stamp duty, added up before you sit in front of a bank.

How a purchase runs here

Three stages, and the portal carries a job in each one.

01

Shortlist on facts

Filter on carpet area, floor, facing and possession date. Save what survives, compare up to three side by side.

02

Check before you travel

Floor plan, tower position, the RERA field, distance to the metro and the schools. A site visit should confirm, not discover.

03

Price the whole thing

Run the EMI at your own rate and tenure, and see registration and GST in the same table as the base price.
