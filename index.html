Questions

Before you get in touch

What does a website cost?
A business website starts at $500. eCommerce, custom portals and web applications are priced against scope — you get a fixed quote after the discovery call, not a range that moves later.
How long does a build take?
A standard business site runs two to four weeks. Mobile apps take six to twelve weeks depending on complexity. AI builds usually land between three and six weeks. You get the timeline in writing before we start.
Can you add AI to software we already have?
Yes — that is most of the AI work we do. We connect to your existing CRM, helpdesk, accounting or internal tools rather than asking you to move to a new platform.
Do you support small businesses without in-house IT?
That is who we are built for. Helpdesk, on-site visits, hardware and software setup, Microsoft 365 management, network configuration and security — at a fraction of the cost of hiring internally.
What happens after launch?
Monthly support covers monitoring, updates, backups and improvements. It is a rolling plan — no lock-in, cancel with a month's notice.
