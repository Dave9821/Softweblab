Before you order

Will the Glow Oil break me out?

It is formulated as a light, non-greasy oil, but any new oil deserves a two-day test on a small patch of skin behind the ear. If you use a prescription retinoid, apply on alternate nights to start.

How do refills work?

Order a pouch instead of a bottle from any product page. Rinse and air-dry your glass bottle, then decant. Pouches cost about 30% less and use 74% less plastic.

Where do you ship, and how fast?

All India. Metros get it in 2–3 working days, the rest in 4–6. Orders above ₹799 ship free, and cash on delivery is available on every pin code we serve.

How should I store it?

Away from direct sun, cap closed. Cold-pressed oils keep best somewhere cool and dark, and the glass bottle is there to help.
