CASE / 01 Professional services AI & AUTOMATION

An assistant that reads years of internal policy

The problem

Staff were losing several minutes on every client question, digging through hundreds of policy PDFs to find one clause. New joiners took months to get confident, and answers varied depending on who you asked.

What we built
  • A private assistant trained on the full document archive, with no data leaving their tenancy
  • Every answer returns the source document and clause, so nothing is taken on trust
  • Slack and browser access, so it sits where the team already works
  • An admin view showing what people ask most — which fed their next FAQ update

CASE / 03 Education PLATFORM

A learner portal built to survive growth

The problem

Course material lived in shared drives and messaging groups. Students couldn't find the right paper, staff re-sent the same files weekly, and there was no way to see who was actually keeping up.

What we built
  • Course boards organised by subject, paper and tier, matching how the syllabus actually works
  • Per-student progress tracking visible to both learner and tutor
  • Role separation for admin, tutor and student, with permissions enforced at the database
  • Structured so new subjects are a content change, not a rebuild

CASE / 04 Schools group PLATFORM

One ERP, many schools, separated data

The problem

A group running several campuses on separate spreadsheets and desktop software. Admissions, attendance, fees and reporting were reconciled by hand at month end, and no two campuses did it the same way.

What we built
  • A multi-tenant platform where each campus sees only its own data, enforced at the database layer
  • Admissions, attendance, fees, staff and reporting in one place
  • Group-level dashboards that roll every campus into a single view
  • Audit trails on financial records, so month end became a report instead of a project

CASE / 06 Multi-branch retail AI & AUTOMATION

Every missed call turned into a booked appointment

The problem

Roughly a third of inbound calls went unanswered during busy hours and after close. Nobody knew what those callers wanted, and most never rang back.

What we built
  • A voice agent that answers overflow and out-of-hours calls in a natural conversation
  • Answers common questions on hours, pricing and location, then books the slot
  • A transcript and summary of every call delivered to the branch inbox
  • Anything outside its scope is handed to a human with full context attached
